Design Direction Overview

Three visual approaches for competition-platform, all built on the same foundation: Inter + JetBrains Mono, 4px grid, zinc palette, shadcn/ui + Tremor components. The differences are in tone, density, and color temperature.

A: Control Room

Dark • Ultra-Dense • Icon Sidebar
RECOMMENDED
  • Collapsed icon-only sidebar (56px)
  • Maximum data density on dashboard
  • Pure zinc palette, blue accent
  • Grafana / Linear inspired
  • Highest information-per-pixel ratio
  • Dark mode native — feels at home in dim venues
Best for: Organizers who want to see everything at once. Matches "mission control" and "tight & efficient" requirements.

B: Warm Ops

Dark • Stone Palette • Amber Accent
ALTERNATIVE
  • Expanded sidebar with labels (220px)
  • Stone/warm neutral palette instead of zinc
  • Amber accent for primary actions
  • Slightly warmer, more approachable
  • Judge cards instead of rows for status
  • Same density, warmer personality
Best for: If the pure zinc/blue feels too cold or technical. Adds warmth without sacrificing professionalism.

C: Light Professional

Light • Clean • Traditional Layout
ALTERNATIVE
  • Expanded sidebar with labels (240px)
  • White cards on light gray background
  • Traditional SaaS dashboard aesthetic
  • Table-based judge status (vs cards/rows)
  • Badge-based status indicators
  • Familiar to anyone who's used Stripe/Linear
Best for: If organizers work in well-lit offices pre-event. Light mode as default with dark mode toggle for venues.

Direction A: Control Room

Dark, dense, icon sidebar. Maximum information density with zinc palette and blue accent. Inspired by Grafana, Linear, and mission control interfaces.
