Infra Services Dashboard

competition-platform · third-party services · pricing, status & metrics
How to keep this current. Everything is driven by the SERVICES array near the top of this file’s <script>. Edit a service’s status, price, or metrics and reload — no build step. Status values: operational · degraded · down. Prices are list prices in USD; usage-metered services (Fly, Upstash) show an estimate. Metric values are placeholders until wired to each provider’s API/console — links per card.
